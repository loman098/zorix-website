How we build

Evidence over hype.

Trading software should be evaluated on its rules, risks and observed behaviour—not just attractive marketing.

01

Define the rules

Document entries, exits, position sizing and the conditions that prevent a trade.

02

Test systematically

Use historical and demo forward testing while accounting for spreads, slippage and execution limits.

03

Report honestly

Publish test dates, settings, drawdown and limitations when reliable reports are available.

Risk disclosure

Know the risks before trading.

Forex and Gold trading can result in rapid and substantial losses. Automated strategies may behave differently across brokers, spreads, execution conditions and market regimes. Backtests do not guarantee future performance.

ZORIX does not promise profits or risk-free trading. Test software on a demo account and understand its settings before considering live use.
